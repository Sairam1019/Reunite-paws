Training Tips for Your Pets

Basic Commands

  • Sit: Hold a treat above your pet's head and move it back, causing them to sit.
  • Stay: Ask your pet to sit, then show a palm and say "stay". Gradually increase the duration.
  • Come: Use a cheerful tone and say "come" while gently pulling your pet towards you.
  • Leave It: Hold a treat in your closed fist and say "leave it". Reward when they stop trying.
  • Heel: Encourage your pet to walk beside you without pulling on the leash.

Positive Reinforcement

Always use positive reinforcement techniques. Reward good behavior with treats, praise, and affection. Avoid punishment as it can lead to fear and aggression.

Consistency is Key

Be consistent with commands and rules. Ensure all family members use the same commands and rewards to avoid confusion.

Short and Fun Sessions

Keep training sessions short (5-10 minutes) and fun to maintain your pet's interest and prevent frustration.
