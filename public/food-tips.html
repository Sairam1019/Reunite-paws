Food Tips for Pets

Best Foods for Pets

  • Chicken: A great source of protein.
  • Rice: Easy to digest and good for stomach issues.
  • Carrots: Low in calories and good for dental health.
  • Sweet Potatoes: Rich in vitamins and fiber.
  • Fish: High in omega-3 fatty acids for a healthy coat.

Foods to Avoid

  • Chocolate: Toxic to pets, can cause severe health issues.
  • Grapes and Raisins: Can cause kidney failure.
  • Onions and Garlic: Can damage red blood cells.
  • Avocado: Contains persin, which can be harmful to pets.
  • Alcohol: Highly toxic, even small amounts can be fatal.
